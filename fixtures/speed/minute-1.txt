Good morning, this is the Thursday note for the Pebble Island ferry crew. The first boat leaves the north dock at seven forty, and the tide table says the channel will be shallow until about nine, so please keep to the marked lane past the sandbar. Marta has the new paper tickets in the blue box by the ramp, and the card reader is charged. We expect two school groups before noon, about thirty children each, with four teachers, so set out the extra life jackets from the lower shelf. The cafe on the island asked us to carry twelve crates of apples and one box of lanterns for the harvest fair on Saturday. Lunch break is staggered again, first the deck team at twelve, then the ticket desk at one. If the fog comes back in the afternoon, slow to half speed near the lighthouse and ring the bell twice at the point. Thanks everyone, and remember to sign the log before you go home.
